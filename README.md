# Weigh It, Pour It – Capacity Games (Class 4 Maths, Chapter 8)

Interactive smartboard games for NCERT Class 4 Maths Chapter 8 *Weigh It, Pour It* (Measuring Capacity).

- **Capacity Sort (page 122)** – sort containers into less than 1 litre, 1 litre and more than 1 litre.
- **Fill the 1 Litre Bottle (pages 122–125)** – pour from 500, 250, 200, 100, 50 and 10 ml containers with a live calculation (sum, grouped multiplication, amount still needed, fractions of a litre), plus challenges.
- **Milk Cans & Leaky Tap (page 127)** – serve customers with milk measuring cans (1000, 500, 200, 100, 50 ml) and calculate water lost from a dripping tap in a day and a week.

Single self-contained `index.html` — no internet libraries needed.
